## 0.1.0

+ New: Initial Pro tier add-on extracted from `MageMe_EUWithdrawal` (Lite) phase-32 split refactor (2026-04-30). Adds forensic-grade durable-medium receipt verification on top of Lite's email receipt: SHA-256 content hash printed in receipt email + public `/withdraw-contract/verify/index/` endpoint for tamper-evidence verification. Pro DI binds `MageMe\EUWithdrawal\Api\Receipt\ContentHasherInterface` to local `ContentHasher` implementation, injects it into Lite's `RequestFinalizer` and `ReceiptSendConsumer` so hash is computed on finalize and validated on send. Lite alone (without this Pro module) writes NULL to `mm_eu_withdrawal_request.content_hash` and the receipt email's integrity-hash table + verify button blocks are hidden via `{{depend content_hash}}` wrappers.
